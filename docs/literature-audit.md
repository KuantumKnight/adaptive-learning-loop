## Revision rule

The revised deck may cite rows marked verified after a team member checks the linked primary record and confirms that the slide's summary is faithful. Rows marked missing or implemented but unverified remain in the audit trail but must be replaced or removed. ## Audit limitation

This is a citation audit, not a human content sign-off. The team still needs to perform the final reference check, record who checked each retained source, and update the presentation only after the check.
